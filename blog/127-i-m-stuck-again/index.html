I'm stuck again.

            #life #update
          

          
I wish I was better at writing these, I feel as if I lost a certain personality within them...


I wish I had more discipline.


I wish my body let me learn more, new things.


I wish my body let me enjoy things.


I wish I could play games again.


I wish I could watch shows again.


I wish I was healthier.


I wish I didn't have to constantly face fear.


I wish I didn't have to constantly face impending doom.


I wish I knew what was happening to my brain.


It's so odd, from memory, looking back at how I wrote earlier this year, to last year, to 2024, 2023, and 2022, I've changed so much. I can't even predict where I'm going to be in the future anymore like I used to. (I used to very accurately simulate future "positions" of me, my interactions, my brain state, my emotions, etc). This is surely uncharted territory!


Although I might aswell be staring at a blank wall all day, apart from me building my game, I do absolutely nothing. I can't read, I can't study, I can barely listen to music, I can only talk to people when they adore me, I can't even enjoy games apart from nostalgia. I can barely learn. It feels like my psyche is about to make a massive shift like it did when I was in my teens, I hope for the better. I do hope I get my pre-2018 drive for knowledge and self-improvement back... I wonder if this constant pressure in my head and tiredness I feel is connected. Even my physical senses are dulled. I don't think this is just burnout? Maybe my body is just catching up to the trauma. Even my memories are heavily affected right now...


I also wish to experience love someday, but I'm sapio/demisexual(bpd) so it's very hard, not to mention that I'm a hikikomori. 13~ years without going outside... How do I even meet people that might even like me? (As my brain tells me that's never going to happen.)


I also wish to experience love someday, without feeling like I'm gross, ugly, unwanted, not good enough, wasted-space, autistic, or too "man". I've never been told a compliment, nor any other romantic thing, even if I'm almost 30. I know some people in my family that'd get a kick out of that and stroke their own ego, but whatever.


In my restless dreams.
