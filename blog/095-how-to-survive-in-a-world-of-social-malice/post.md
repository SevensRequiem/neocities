i suppose it's also time to dissect my internal coping/defensive mechanisms
how i survived over the years is:

1. internalizing a "knowing" that i am un-wanted/un-loved by any and all
this has been very freeing, it has also killed my general anxieties with people

2. thanks to many people in the past 15 years performing reactive abuse & trying to get me to commit suicide, alongside 1, i weaponized this and used it to kill my own suicidal ideation.

3. chameleon circuit! 

4. internalizing my intellect; an outer mask that speaks for me
this has been a bit troubling lately as ive out-grown it, and i have no need for it anymore. i have 0 need to mask who i am thanks to #1 and #2. 

5. learning to regulate my fight/flight responses & during ptsd taking over my day dreams (mal-adaptive/simulations) with high paced games & zoning out with music (fight / flight), each activates a different system, and I'm doing my best to train my body to not be so afraid of certain actions, or series of actions. 




i felt a need to write this as someone from my past flooded my mind (maladaptive daydreaming) while doing #5. it was making me zone out alot. the person was an ex abusive "best friend" narcissistic person. and the whole "idea" of that daydream was him realizing he's done me wrong & him turning people against me abroad, even in germany, was wrong. i wonder if this is some part of bpd i havent realized yet, or even ptsd, my brain trying to get me to let go?
