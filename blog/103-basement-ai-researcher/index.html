My current project "Xa" in honor of XaTuring, has led me to some interesting foundational theories on how a living AI should be Architected. I'm looking into how to hash and store every neural network tick, so any future instance of this NN can compute the hash to get its memory. Around 8 to 9 gb / day of disk writes in testing without compression. I dub it Neural Network Time of Compute or NNTC for short. I'll probably post an example of it on github under something like "Identify historical execution boundaries and reconstruct their complete declared state". The hash will store the exact state of a neural net at a current tick at a specific unix timestamp down to exact sensory inputs. I believe this will be needed in an Alive system!
