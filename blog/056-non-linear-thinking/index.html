non-linear thinking

          

          
through my self-reflection i have found out that my way of thinking is non-linear. it is why i struggle to write blog posts, finish tasks, or projects. who knows if this has a clinical name but my thinking often goes from A to D to metaphysical Z (not bound by conceptual boundaries, I usually hold multiple contradictions in my mind at any given time) i could be thinking about theism, polytheism, monotheism, atheism, and spirituality all simultaneously. it is very taxing and i attribute it to my constant fatigue, it wont turn off! and trying to write here, linearly, is such a pain. maybe i should develop a writing style that starts at G, goes to A, then D, then Z! it would certainly fit me and my mind better.
