I'm unsure on what else to add to the site, I usually just remake the theme every other month in a restless fashion. I would like to stop doing that, but I'm not entirely "solid"?? My mind flows too freely, it's an annoyance in meditations sometimes, but after 8 or so years of meditations it's gotten easier.
