Exhaustion.

            #life #mental-health
          

          
I'm lucky if I get to watch an episode in a day for an anime, or even a youtube video. I get bored and tired mid way through anything anymore and I blame cptsd/hypervigilance. A wrong noise outside, a tree twig falling, an acorn hitting the roof, and boom instant adrenaline. I hate living like this but when I've never really been allowed to live, the accumulated stress and trauma just kinda has my body at a max. I do hope I get to live soon without struggle, I'm tired man. I want comfortable friends, I want to do comfortable things, I want to enjoy even gaming again without feeling the need to sleep 30 minutes in. Thank god for caffeine (800-1000mg/day so far).


Ughhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhh I hate being mentally ill, I'm so tired of ittttt. At least my suicidal ideation hasn't been a thing for a couple years!
