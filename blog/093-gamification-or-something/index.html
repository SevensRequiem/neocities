now that ive healed some, that has shifted to adrenaline chasing in fps games, most notably war-thunder high br jets, it's fun, and i'm actively improving. theres been instances where i went 1v6 and won in only 10s. F/A 18C vs SU30m / Mirage / Typhoon / Mig 29 / F/A 18E. it was pretty fun and very stimulating, but after the dump i noticed that my regulation kicked in and tried to counter-act the adrenaline. interesting process & i believe its helping me heal years of adrenaline dumping from ptsd / irl situations.
